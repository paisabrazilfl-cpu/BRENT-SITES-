Do I Qualify?

You May Qualify If:

  • Your child used Roblox while under 18.
  • Your child was contacted by an unsafe adult or predator.
  • Your child experienced exploitation, grooming, threats, coercion, or emotional harm.
  • You reported the issue or have messages/screenshots.
  • The incident caused distress, counseling needs, school disruption, or family harm.
Qualification depends on individual facts and attorney review. This website does not guarantee compensation or legal representation.
